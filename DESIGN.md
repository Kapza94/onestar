# OneStar design

Scene: a founder at 11pm in a dim kitchen. The laptop is the only lamp. Complaints get circled in cadmium red. Gaps get tabbed in acid lime.

Color strategy: full palette.
Reference: forensic lab under tungsten, not “dark SaaS.”

## Color (OKLCH)

- Graphite ground: `oklch(0.145 0.014 52)`
- Raised paper: `oklch(0.185 0.016 52)`
- Warm off-white type: `oklch(0.93 0.02 85)`
- Rage: `oklch(0.64 0.195 32)`
- Acid opportunity: `oklch(0.84 0.185 132)`
- Hairline: `oklch(0.28 0.016 52)`

## Type

- UI and headings: Bricolage Grotesque (lab equipment, slightly irregular)
- Quoted complaints: Source Serif 4 (logbook excerpts)
- Stamps and counts: Archivo Narrow

## Surfaces

Landing is asymmetric: huge tagline, intake sheet on the right.
Wall of Rage is energetic, slightly staggered, red-warm.
Blueprint is calmer, more air, acid marks, numbered actions.
No glass, no gradient text, no left-accent stripes, no icon-card grids.
