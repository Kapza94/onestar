# OneStar

Register: brand on the intake surface, product on the report.

## Product purpose

OneStar turns competitors’ worst public reviews and complaints into an evidence-backed product strategy. It is a one-sitting research lab for founders, not a sentiment dashboard.

## Users

A founder with a half-formed idea, a laptop, and a night. They may not know their competitors. They need a specific thing to build, not a market-size slide.

## Brand voice

Forensic, tungsten, acid-tabbed. The physical object is a lab logbook: graphite paper, red grease-pencil circling complaints, acid-green tabs on the pages that matter.

## Anti-references

Generic purple AI gradients. Glassmorphism. Enterprise heatmap dashboards. “AI insight” cards with identical icons. Invented 1-star reviews. Claims of statistical significance.

## Strategic principles

- Every factual claim has a source link.
- Frequency means this collected sample, never the whole market.
- Unknown stays unknown.
- Example data is labeled Example data. Live research never silently substitutes fiction.
- Advice must be specific to the submitted idea and the complaints found.
